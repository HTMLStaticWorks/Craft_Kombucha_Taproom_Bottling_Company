01 · OUR STORY

Tea. Culture.
Time.

FROM LEAF
TO FIZZ

We started KOVA with one simple idea: a fermented drink should feel as good as the place you enjoy it in.

We brew premium tea, let our culture do its work, then layer in real fruits, herbs and spices. Every batch gets time, attention and a final taste before it reaches the tap.
